# Researching the TV anime CUE!

Open *CUE!* from Annict's [Winter 2022 list](https://annict.com/works/2022-winter?display=list_detailed). Confirm the TV format and season, then follow the official site, Cast, or Episodes link.

## Search examples

- Search Pixiv Encyclopedia with `site:dic.pixiv.net/a "CUE!" アニメ`, `site:dic.pixiv.net/a "六石陽菜"`, or `site:dic.pixiv.net/a "CUE!" "ハムレット"`, using the terms that match the question. If you cannot confirm a matching article, continue with Annict and the official sources. Check the version because results can include the source game.
- Find the work with `site:annict.com/works "CUE!" 2022年冬` or `キュー TVアニメ 2022`.
- Find episode 1 with `"CUE!" "第1話" "はじまりのはじまり"`. For an unknown episode, try `"CUE!" "六石陽菜" "ハムレット" 何話` and check the candidate against Annict and a source that describes the scene.
- Find cast with `site:cue-animation.jp/staff "六石陽菜"` and the name reading with `site:liberent.co.jp "CUE!" "六石陽菜" TVアニメ`.

The official CHARACTER and STORY pages may show little content in text extraction. Try the rendered pages. The licensed episode listings can support the events they describe. STAFF / CAST and the Liber announcement can support cast and readings if the character page is unavailable.

The Liber announcement covers the game and events as well. Use its TV anime details section and the link labeled アニメ公式HP. The domain `cue-liber.jp` is for the source game.
